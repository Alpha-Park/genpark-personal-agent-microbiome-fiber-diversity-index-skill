# Personal Agent Microbiome Fiber Diversity Index Skill

Designed for **OpenClaw**, **Hermes**, and autonomous personal AI agents to optimize gut-brain axis communication, quantify prebiotic fiber entropy via the Shannon-Wiener index, track the American Gut Project 30-plant weekly benchmark, and project Short-Chain Fatty Acid (SCFA butyrate) production.

## Agent In-Context Capabilities

- **30-Plant Weekly Biodiversity Ledger**: Tracks rolling distinct botanical species across Alliums, Cruciferous, Legumes, Whole Grains, Berries, Roots, Nuts, and Herbs.
- **Shannon-Wiener Entropy Modeling**: Measures whether fiber intake is evenly distributed across multiple distinct polysaccharide families.
- **SCFA & Butyrate Estimation**: Correlates prebiotic diversity with colonic epithelial tight junction integrity and suppressed systemic endotoxemia.

## Tools Summary

1. `calculate_fiber_diversity_index`: Computes species count, Shannon H', and SCFA output tier.
2. `recommend_microbiome_substrate_additions`: Recommends highest-leverage missing prebiotic botanical substrates.
